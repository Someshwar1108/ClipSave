What's new in V3.8

  • Stays in your menu bar — ClipSave now opens at login by default and reopens itself if macOS stops it. Quit from the menu still keeps it closed until your next login
  • Safe on a full disk — if your Mac runs out of space, ClipSave keeps working, shows a small warning, and saves again as soon as space is free
