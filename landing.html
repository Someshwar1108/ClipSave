⌘⇧V Open ClipSave from any app
⌘1–9 Instantly copy clip 1 through 9
↑ ↓ Navigate clips with arrow keys
↩ Enter Copy selected clip and close
⌫ Delete Remove the selected clip
⌘F Focus the search bar
⌘, Open Settings
Esc Close ClipSave
How it works

Three steps.
That's it.

Install, copy, paste. ClipSave handles the rest.

Copy as usual

Keep using ⌘C the way you always have. ClipSave runs silently in your menu bar, capturing everything you copy.

Summon anywhere

Press ⌘⇧V from any app. The panel appears next to your cursor with your clip history.

Paste instantly

Click or press ⌘1-9 to grab the clip you want. It's on your clipboard, ready to paste with ⌘V.
