Features

Everything you copy,
always within reach

Built natively for macOS with the polish you'd expect from a top-tier indie Mac app.

Multi-type capture

Text, rich text, images, screenshots, and files — ClipSave catches everything you copy with full fidelity.

Global hotkey

Press ⌘⇧V from anywhere on your Mac. The panel appears instantly under your cursor. Fully customizable in Settings.

100% local

Nothing leaves your Mac. No cloud, no sync, no telemetry. Your clipboard is yours.

Keyboard-first

Arrow keys to navigate. ⌘1-9 to grab clip N instantly. Press once, paste anywhere.

Pin forever

Important snippets? Pin them to the permanent tab. Your saved clips persist across launches.

Screenshot ready

Auto-captures screenshots saved to Desktop. Real thumbnails. Hover to preview at full size.

Light + dark

Beautifully adapts to your system theme. Looks native in both modes.

Auto-paste

Pick a clip → it pastes directly into your previous app. Optional, off by default.

Auto-updates

New versions install silently in the background. Always on the latest, never lifting a finger.

Power user

Keyboard shortcuts
built for speed

Every action one keystroke away. No mouse needed.
Every shortcut below — plus a few optional ones — can be reassigned in Settings.

⌘⇧V Open ClipSave from any app
⌘1–9 Instantly copy clip 1 through 9
↑ ↓ Navigate clips with arrow keys
↩ Enter Copy selected clip and close
⌫ Delete Remove the selected clip
⌘F Focus the search bar
⌘, Open Settings
Esc Close ClipSave
How it works

Three steps.
That's it.

Install, copy, paste. ClipSave handles the rest.

Copy as usual

Keep using ⌘C the way you always have. ClipSave runs silently in your menu bar, capturing everything you copy.

Summon anywhere

Press ⌘⇧V from any app. The panel appears next to your cursor with your clip history.

Paste instantly

Click or press ⌘1-9 to grab the clip you want. It's on your clipboard, ready to paste with ⌘V.

Start keeping every copy in 10 seconds.
