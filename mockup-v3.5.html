6 · Settings → Shortcuts The Shortcuts tab becomes editable instead of a static reference list: a recorder field for the global hotkey, plus the two persistence toggles. The read-only cheat-sheet you already have stays below, scrolled.

Two things I need your call on

  • Vertical space. The chips row costs ~30 pt of a 480 pt panel, and the list already only shows ~5 rows. I'd recommend collapsing the Add to temp… field into a + button next to the chips — it's the least-used control in the panel and buys the row back. Say the word and I'll mock that variant, or I can grow the panel to 320 × 540 instead.
  • Color detection scope. The mockup shows #RRGGBB and a named color. Do you want rgb()/rgba()/hsl() too, and should a color clip offer “copy as hex / rgb” conversions? That's a small parser either way, just want to size it right.

Not drawn, because they aren't visual: persistence-across-restart (toggle shown in 6), and the v3.4 bugs I found earlier — the thumbnail rebuild, the tempClips.first-only dedup, the 64-byte image equality key, and Delete-in-search-field deleting a clip. I'd fix those in the same pass since I'm touching the same code.
